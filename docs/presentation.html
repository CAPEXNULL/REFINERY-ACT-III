06 / CREDIBILITY

Make the limits visible.

What the prototype demonstrates

  • A working synthetic process simulation and operator interface.
  • Observation-based hypothesis ranking and action forecasts.
  • Persisted results, engineering review, and report exports.
  • A limited A/B/C synthetic model experiment with integrity checks.

What still needs validation

  • Similarity scores are not calibrated probabilities.
  • Forecasts depend on model assumptions.
  • Imported data has unverified provenance.
  • Real-plant calibration, authenticated integration, and industrial validation remain future work.
Missing observations remain missing. Display alarm settings do not change physical protection.
